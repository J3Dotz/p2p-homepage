Data Protection Risk Assessments

A single, defined piece of work that tells you where your real exposure is and what to do about it, in priority order.

What you get

A fixed-scope engagement with named deliverables, typically four to six weeks end to end.

01

Risk register

Every identified risk scored for likelihood and impact, in one sheet your board can work from.

02

Gap analysis against UK GDPR

Where current practice sits against each obligation, with evidence noted.

03

Prioritised remediation plan

Fixes sequenced by risk, each with an owner, an effort estimate and a date.

04

Findings walkthrough

A session with your team so the report doesn't become a document nobody opens.

Who it's for

—Organisations that need a defensible view of risk for a board, funder or insurer.
—Teams introducing a new system or supplier and unsure what it changes.
—Anyone who has had a near miss and wants to know what else is exposed.
