Outsourced DPO Services

A named data protection officer for organisations that need the role filled properly but don't need it filled full time.

What the role covers

Everything the UK GDPR expects of a DPO, carried out by someone who knows your organisation rather than a rota of unfamiliar names.

Monitoring and advice

We track how you're using personal data as things change, and flag issues before they become findings.

Requests and incidents

Subject access requests, complaints and breaches handled with you, to the statutory deadlines.

ICO contact point

A registered point of contact for the regulator, and support with any correspondence that arrives.

Records and reporting

Your records of processing kept current, plus a short report your board or trustees can actually read.

Small management team round a table, mid-discussion — three or four people, candid

Who it's for

—Organisations legally required to appoint a DPO, including public authorities and schools.
—SMEs handling special category data at scale, where the role can't sit with an already-stretched manager.
—Teams that need genuine independence in the role, which an internal appointment can struggle to provide.
Not sure whether you're required to appoint one? The free audit answers that in writing.
