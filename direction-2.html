What compliance actually involves, sector by sector

01 Schools & academies Pupil records · MIS · SARs +

Safeguarding files, pupil records held across MIS and cloud tools, photography consent, and subject access requests from separated parents. We work to the school calendar and brief governors in language they can act on.

Typical engagement: assessment plus termly support Schools guidance →
02 Healthcare & care Special category · sharing · DSPT +

Lawful bases for health data, sharing with local authorities and family, retention schedules that survive an inspection, and the Data Security and Protection Toolkit without the last-minute scramble.

Typical engagement: assessment plus retained cover Healthcare guidance →
03 Hospitality & leisure Bookings · CCTV · marketing +

Booking platforms and payment processors, CCTV signage and retention, loyalty schemes, and marketing consent that holds up across several sites and a seasonal team.

Typical engagement: assessment plus staff training Hospitality guidance →
04 SaaS & technology Processor duties · DPAs · transfers +

Processor obligations, data processing agreements you can sign without a lawyer each time, international transfer paperwork, and the security questionnaires enterprise buyers send before they'll commit.

Typical engagement: assessment plus buyer-readiness pack SaaS guidance →
